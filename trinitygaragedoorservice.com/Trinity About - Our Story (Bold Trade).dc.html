Local, And We Mean It

Not A Chain With A Local Sounding Name

When you call Trinity, you reach people who live in the same towns you do. We drive the same roads, sit in the same traffic, and sweat through the same Florida summers that wear your door out. So when we look at your garage, we're thinking about how it holds up here, not in some other state.

A lot of our customers end up talking to Jason, the owner, directly. He answers questions straight, and he'll tell you when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, even when the bigger job would pay us more. That kind of honesty is the whole reason people call us back and send their neighbors our way.

We Know Florida Doors

Built For Heat, Humidity & Salt Air

Heat, humidity, and salt air are rough on springs and hardware, and storm season is rough on the door itself. We've spent years working on doors in exactly these conditions, so we know which parts hold up around here and which ones quit early. When we fix or install a door, we do it with our weather in mind.

A Few Questions We Hear A Lot

Frequently Asked

Are you really family owned?
Yes. Trinity is family owned and run right here in Tampa Bay. We're not a franchise of a national brand.
Do you charge for estimates?
No. Estimates are free. We'll come look at the job and give you a real number with no obligation to book.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Florida under GD13010 and GDI-09484.
Do you offer emergency service?
We do, 24/7. A broken spring or a door stuck halfway doesn't wait for business hours, so neither do we.
How soon can you come out?
Often the same day. Call early and there's a good chance we can have a technician at your house within a couple of hours.
