# Service Areas

Trinity is based right here in Tampa Bay, and we cover a good stretch of it. If your garage door quits in Hillsborough, Pasco, or Pinellas county, there's a strong chance we can be at your house the same day. Here's where we work and how to tell if you're in range.

## The towns we cover

We focus on six towns and the neighborhoods around each one. Find yours below to see what we handle in your area.

* **[Lutz](/service-areas/lutz/).** Established neighborhoods and lakefront homes about fifteen miles north of downtown Tampa.
* **[Land O Lakes](/service-areas/land-o-lakes/).** Pasco lake country, where newer master planned communities sit next to older waterfront homes.
* **[Wesley Chapel](/service-areas/wesley-chapel/).** One of the fastest growing parts of the bay, full of newer homes along the interstate.
* **[Palm Harbor](/service-areas/palm-harbor/).** A Gulf coast community on St. Joseph Sound, where salt air is rough on door hardware.
* **[Oldsmar](/service-areas/oldsmar/).** A bayfront city at the head of Old Tampa Bay, with plenty of waterfront and canal homes.
* **[Tampa](/service-areas/tampa/).** The heart of the bay, where historic Hyde Park and Ybor sit alongside new construction and waterfront South Tampa.

## Not sure you're in our area?

If your town isn't on the list, call us anyway. We cover a lot of the ground in between these places, including spots like Odessa, Carrollwood, Westchase, and New Tampa, and we'll tell you straight whether we can get to you.

## Why your neighbors call us

We're family owned and local, so you get a real person on the phone and a technician who knows the area. We tell you what's actually wrong, give you a clear price before we start, and most repairs get done the same day. We're licensed, bonded, and insured in Florida under GD13010 and GDI-09484.
