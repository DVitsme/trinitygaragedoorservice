## Repairs

**A spring broke. Can I still use the door?**
Please don't. With a spring gone, the door is far heavier than the opener was built to lift, and running it can burn out the motor or bring the door down hard. Leave it closed and call us. More on our [spring repair](/services/repair/spring/) page.

**Do I have to replace both springs if only one broke?**
On a two spring door, we recommend it. Both springs are the same age and the same amount of worn, so doing both keeps the door balanced and saves you a second service call soon. On a single spring door, we just replace the one.

**How long do springs and openers last?**
Springs usually run about seven to ten years and openers about ten to fifteen, depending on how often you use the door. Our heat, humidity, and salt air can shorten both, so don't be surprised if yours wear a little sooner.

**My door came off the track. What should I do?**
Stop using it. Don't hit the opener and don't try to force it back, since the door can fall or do more damage. Leave it be and call us. Here's our [off track repair](/services/repair/off-track/) page.

**Why is my garage door so loud?**
Usually worn rollers, dry hinges, and loose hardware. Switching to nylon or sealed bearing rollers and a good tune up quiets most doors right down. See [cable and roller repair](/services/repair/cables-rollers/) and [tune up and maintenance](/services/repair/tune-up/).

**Can you fix a brand you didn't install?**
Yes. We service and repair every major door and opener brand, even ones we don't sell new.

## New doors and replacement

**Should I repair my door or replace it?**
It depends on the door's age and what's wrong. We'll give you an honest read and never push a new door when a repair will do. Our [replacement](/services/replacement/) page walks through how to make the call.

**How long does a new door installation take?**
Most single doors go in within a day. Bigger, custom, or double doors can take longer. We'll give you the timeline when we quote it.

**Do I need a permit for a new door?**
In most of Tampa Bay, yes, and the permit confirms the door meets the wind rating for your area. We pull it and handle the inspection.

**Does my new door have to be wind rated?**
Much of Tampa Bay is a wind borne debris zone, so your opening usually needs a door rated for the pressure at your address. We'll tell you what your home requires before you buy. See our [door types and styles](/doors/types/) page.

**Does a new door come with an opener?**
The opener is separate from the door. If yours still works well, we can reconnect it. If it's old or you want smart features, we can install a new one.

## Doors and brands

**Which brands do you carry?**
We install Clopay, C.H.I., Hörmann, and Amarr doors and LiftMaster openers, and we service and repair every major brand. See our [brands](/doors/brands/) page.

**What door material is best for Florida?**
Insulated steel is the all around favorite. Right on the coast, aluminum and composite hold up better against salt and humidity. Wood is beautiful but needs the most upkeep in our climate.
