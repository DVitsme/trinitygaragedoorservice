# Our Story

Trinity Garage Door Service is a family owned company, and we've kept Tampa Bay's garage doors moving since 2007. The idea behind the company has always been simple. Show up when we say we will, tell you the truth about your door, and charge a fair price for good work.

That's it. No call center, no runaround, no script to read off of.

## Local, and we mean it

We're not a national chain with a local sounding name. When you call Trinity, you reach people who live in the same towns you do. We drive the same roads, sit in the same traffic, and sweat through the same Florida summers that wear your door out. So when we look at your garage, we're thinking about how it holds up here, not in some other state.

A lot of our customers end up talking to Jason, the owner, directly. He answers questions straight, and he'll tell you when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, even when the bigger job would pay us more. That kind of honesty is the whole reason people call us back and send their neighbors our way.

## How we work

We keep it plain.

* We tell you what's actually wrong, not what's easiest to sell.
* You get a clear price before any work starts, so the number never moves on you.
* We do the job right, test it, and clean up before we leave.

If a spring will fix it, we fix the spring. If the door's really past saving, we show you why and walk you through the options without leaning on you.

## The crew

Our technicians have been doing this for years, and our customers tend to remember them by name. Read through our reviews and you'll see the same folks come up again and again, David and Joey among them, usually right next to words like honest and professional. We take that personally, in a good way.

## What we do

We work on the whole door, the quick repairs and the full installs both.

* Garage door repair, including springs, openers, cables, rollers, and doors that have jumped the track
* New installation and full door replacement
* 24/7 emergency repair for when your door quits at the worst possible time

We install and service doors from Clopay, C.H.I., Hormann, and Amarr, along with openers from LiftMaster, Genie, Chamberlain, and others. Our services pages cover each one in detail.

## Where we work

We cover Tampa Bay across Hillsborough, Pasco, and Pinellas counties. That includes Lutz, Land O Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Palm Harbor, Oldsmar, and Tampa, plus the towns in between. Not sure you're in our area? Give us a call and we'll tell you straight.

## Licensed, bonded, and insured

Trinity is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Florida under GD13010 and GDI-09484. We've also earned a BBB A+ rating and the Angi Super Service Award for 2024. Those matter because they mean the work is done to code and you're protected if anything ever goes wrong.

## We know Florida doors

Heat, humidity, and salt air are rough on springs and hardware, and storm season is rough on the door itself. We've spent years working on doors in exactly these conditions, so we know which parts hold up around here and which ones quit early. When we fix or install a door, we do it with our weather in mind.
