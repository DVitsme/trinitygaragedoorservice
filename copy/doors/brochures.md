# Garage Door Brochures

A new garage door is a lot easier to pick when you can actually see the options. These are the manufacturer brochures and spec sheets for the doors and openers we work with, the same catalogs we would hand you at the kitchen table. Have a look, mark what catches your eye, and we will take it from there with a free estimate.

These cover Clopay and C.H.I. doors and LiftMaster openers. If you are after a different brand, call us and we will track down the right information for you. You can also see everyone we work with on our [brands page](/doors/brands/), or read up on styles and materials on our [door types and styles](/doors/types/) page.
