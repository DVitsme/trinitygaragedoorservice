# Garage Door Brands We Carry

Here's some good news: we're not a single brand dealer. We install the door and opener lines we trust and stand behind, and we service and repair just about every brand out there. So when you ask us what to buy, you get an honest answer for your house and your budget, not a pitch for the one product we're stuck selling.

## Doors we install and service

These are the four door brands we install most. All four offer insulated and wind rated models, which matters in our part of Florida.

**Clopay.** The largest residential garage door maker in North America, and our go to for good reason. They make everything, value steel doors and custom faux wood carriage doors alike, so there's a Clopay for almost any house and budget. Ask about their Intellicore insulation for a quiet, well insulated door, and their WindCode rated models for storm season. The Canyon Ridge and Coachman lines nail the carriage look, and the Classic steel series is a solid value.

**C.H.I. Overhead Doors.** A dealer favorite known for solid build quality, made in Illinois. C.H.I. is a great pick if you want the wood look without the wood. Their Accents Woodtones steel doors use a printed grain that holds up to a close look, and their Planks line gives a clean modern wood look. Insulated and wind rated options are available.

**Hörmann.** The premium, design forward choice. Hörmann is a German maker that's built doors since 1935, and the engineering shows in the insulation, the security, and the smooth quiet operation. It costs more and there are fewer dealers around, but if you want a high end door it's worth a look. Hörmann makes their own openers to match.

**Amarr.** A well regarded mainstream brand with a big range of styles and several insulation tiers. Amarr offers hurricane and wind rated models that get specified all over Florida, so it's a dependable, easy choice. The Classica carriage line and the Lincoln traditional line are popular sellers.

## Openers we install and service

**LiftMaster.** The opener we install most, and the one most pros trust. LiftMaster is the professional, dealer installed line, built heavier than the versions you'll find on a store shelf. You get the myQ app to open, close, and check your door from your phone, and battery backup models so the door still works in an outage. When we put in a new opener, this is usually the one.

## Brands we service and repair

We fix doors and openers from every major brand, even the ones we don't sell new. If you've got one of these, we can almost always get the parts and get it running again.

**Chamberlain.** LiftMaster's retail sibling, made by the same company and running the same myQ app. The store bought version is a little lighter duty, and we service and repair it all the time.

**Genie.** A long running opener brand you'll find at the home stores. Genie uses its own Aladdin Connect smart app rather than myQ, which trips some people up. We repair and replace them either way.

**Craftsman.** The familiar Craftsman openers are actually built by the same company that makes Chamberlain and LiftMaster, so they're a known quantity for us to service and find parts for.

**Linear.** Better known for gate openers and access control than household garage openers, Linear shows up on a lot of gated properties. We service their garage operators and can help with gate setups too.

**Wayne Dalton.** Here's one worth clearing up. Wayne Dalton is really a door maker, not an opener brand, and they stopped making openers a while back. If you have an older Wayne Dalton opener like an iDrive, parts are getting scarce, so we'll fix it if we can and talk replacement if we can't. We also service their doors, including the TorqueMaster spring system they're known for.

## A quick word on Florida wind ratings

If you're replacing a door, ask us about wind rated models. Much of Tampa Bay sits in a wind borne debris zone, so your opening needs a door rated for the pressure at your address. Clopay calls their system WindCode, and Amarr, C.H.I., and Hörmann all offer rated lines. Our [door types and styles](/doors/types/) page explains how Florida ratings work, and you can flip through real options in our [brochures](/doors/brochures/).

## Brand questions

**Do I have to pick a brand you install?**
Not at all. If you want a new door or opener, we'll point you to the lines we install and trust. If you already own another brand, we'll service and repair it just the same.

**Which garage door brand is best?**
There's no single best. The right door depends on your house, your budget, and how close you are to the coast. Clopay and Amarr cover most needs really well, C.H.I. is great for a wood look, and Hörmann is the premium pick. We'll help you sort it out.

**Do you only install LiftMaster openers?**
LiftMaster is what we install most, because it's the professional grade line and it holds up. If you have another brand, we service and repair it, and we can talk options if you want something specific.

**Can you fix my opener even if you didn't install it?**
Yes. We repair every major opener brand, including Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, Linear, and older Wayne Dalton units, and we carry common parts on the truck.

**Is Wayne Dalton a door or an opener brand?**
A door brand. They used to make openers but stopped, so if your Wayne Dalton opener dies, replacement parts can be hard to find. We'll fix what we can and help you replace it if we can't.
