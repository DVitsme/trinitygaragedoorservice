# Garage Door Services in Tampa Bay

Trinity is a family owned garage door company working all over Tampa Bay. We install new doors, fix the ones that quit on you, replace the ones past saving, and handle the springs, cables, rollers, tracks, and openers that make a door work. Some of our jobs are planned out. A lot of them are "the door won't open and I have to get to work." We do both.

Here's everything we handle. Find what's going on with your door and click through for the details.

## What we do

### Garage Door Installation
A brand new door, on an existing house or new construction. We help you pick the material, style, and brand that fit your house and your budget, then install it and make sure it runs right. Doors from Clopay, C.H.I., Hormann, and Amarr. [Learn more]

### Garage Door Repair and Service
This is the catch all for a door that's acting up: noisy operation, slow or jerky movement, a remote that won't respond, a door that sticks halfway. We track down the cause, fix it, and can do a tune up while we're there so a small problem doesn't grow into a big one. Often same day. [Learn more]

### Off Track Repair and Replacement
When a door jumps its track it can jam, hang crooked, or come down hard, and that's a real safety risk. We get it back on track or replace bent and damaged track, and we figure out what knocked it off in the first place. Don't fight a door that's off its track; call us. [Learn more]

### Garage Door Replacement
When repairing the old door stops being worth it, we help you decide and then handle the swap. Old door out, new door in, mess hauled away. We'll also tell you straight if it's really time, or if a quick repair will hold you over instead. [Learn more]

### Spring Repair and Replacement
The springs carry the weight of the whole door, so they take a beating and usually last about seven to ten years before they go. A broken spring is the number one reason a door won't open, and it's not a DIY job, those springs are under serious tension. We replace torsion and extension springs and balance the door so it runs easy again. [Learn more]

### Opener Repair and Replacement
If the door is fine but the opener isn't, that's its own fix. We repair motors, sensors, gears, and remotes, and we replace openers that are worn out, usually somewhere around ten to fifteen years old. We work on chain, belt, and screw drive units, and we can set you up with a smart opener you run from your phone. [Learn more]

### When it can't wait
Some things can't sit until business hours, like a broken spring the morning of work or a door stuck wide open overnight. We run a 24/7 emergency line for exactly that. Call it and we'll get someone headed your way.
