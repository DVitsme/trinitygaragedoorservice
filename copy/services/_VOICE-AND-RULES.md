# Trinity Services Copy: Voice & Rules (read this first, every time)

We are writing new copy for Trinity Garage Door Service. Current site: https://trinitygaragedoorservice.com/. Match the company's real voice. Sound like a person, not a chatbot.

## The voice we want (match this)
Warm, honest, plain spoken, local. The way the About page and homepage talk: a family owned Tampa Bay company speaking to a neighbor, not a corporation. Confident but never pushy. Specific about garage doors and about Florida living.

Real anchors from the site and reviews:
- Family owned and local. They live and work in Tampa Bay.
- Honesty first. No high pressure sales. They will not recommend something you do not need. Fair, upfront pricing.
- Show up fast and get it done. Same day help, 24/7 for emergencies.
- Plain and friendly. Talks straight, a little down to earth, sometimes a touch of humor.

## What to AVOID (do not copy this)
The old SERVICE pages are wordy, padded for SEO, and read a bit machine written. Do not imitate that. Keep it tight and real. If a sentence does not earn its place, cut it.

## Hard rules (non negotiable)

1) NO DASHES OF ANY KIND.
   No em dash. No en dash. No hyphen used as a pause or aside. Avoid hyphens even inside compound words when a normal open spelling exists. Write "same day," "off track," "family owned," "high cycle," "wood look," "long term," "24/7." Use a comma, a period, the words "and / so / but," or a fresh sentence instead of a dash. Before you save, scan every file and delete any "—", "–", or stray " - ".

2) DO NOT SOUND LIKE AI. Banned moves:
   - "It is not just X, it is Y."
   - "Whether you need X or Y, we have you covered."
   - "From X to Y, we do it all."
   - Three item flourishes like "fast, reliable, and affordable."
   - Buzzwords: elevate, seamless, robust, cutting edge, state of the art, unparalleled, top notch, peace of mind, rest assured, look no further, in today's world, nestled, boasts, testament, bustling, "ensure" on repeat.
   - Filler openers: "Here's the thing," "That's where we come in," "Look no further."
   - Do not make every paragraph the same length or start every bullet the same way.

3) WRITE LIKE A PERSON.
   Use contractions (we'll, you're, don't, it's). Vary sentence length. Use concrete details: real symptoms, real brands, real towns. Keep paragraphs short, two to four sentences. It is fine to start a sentence with And, So, or But once in a while.

4) BE ACCURATE. DO NOT INVENT.
   No made up prices, warranties, guarantees, or stats. If a number is not in the facts below or in the research, write the benefit without a fake figure. Web search is allowed and encouraged for technical accuracy (spring types, opener drives, why doors go off track) and for the Florida angle (heat, humidity, salt air, storm and hurricane season, wind rated doors). Cite nothing on the page; just be correct.

5) HOUSE STYLE.
   American spelling. Phone (813) 279-6785. Licensed, bonded, insured. License GD13010 and GDI-09484. Serving Tampa Bay: Hillsborough, Pasco, and Pinellas counties, including Lutz, Land O Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Palm Harbor, Oldsmar, and Tampa. Brand color is red. Tagline already on the logo: "Opening Doors Since 2007."

## Facts you can rely on (from discovery research)
- Six services: installation, repair and service, off track repair and replacement, replacement, spring repair and replacement, opener repair and replacement. Plus 24/7 emergency repair.
- Door brands they carry: Clopay, C.H.I., Hormann, Amarr. Opener brands: LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, Linear, Wayne Dalton.
- Door materials: steel, wood, aluminum and glass, composite. Door types: roll up, sectional, side hinged, slide to the side, tilt up.
- Spring life is usually about seven to ten years. Openers usually last about ten to fifteen years. Two spring types: torsion and extension.
- Florida angle worth using naturally: heat, humidity, and salt air wear out springs and hardware faster, and storm season is hard on doors. Do not force it on every page.
- Differentiators that are true: family owned, local, honest and low pressure, fair pricing, fast and same day, licensed and insured, free estimates.
- Payments accepted: cash, check, bank transfer, debit, credit, Google Pay, Zelle.
- Do NOT state a specific warranty length or specific prices. Free estimates are fine to mention.

## Page shape (a guide, not a form to fill robotically)
For each SERVICE DETAIL page:
- H1 and a short intro that names the problem or the job in plain words.
- A section on the signs or situations a customer is dealing with (symptoms, or reasons to install or replace).
- What Trinity actually does on the visit, and what is included.
- A short "what to expect" or "how it works" if it helps.
- Why people call Trinity for this (local, honest, fast, licensed). Brands where relevant.
- A few real FAQs (three to five), answered plainly.
- A clear close with the phone number and a book or estimate prompt.

For the SERVICE OVERVIEW (hub) page:
- A short, human intro to what Trinity does.
- A clear set of the services with two or three honest sentences each, written so someone can self identify and click through.
- Why Trinity, the service area, and a clear close with phone and CTA.

Write meta title and meta description for each page too (no dashes there either).

## Output
One markdown file per page in copy/services/. Start each file with the page title, the URL slug, a meta title, and a meta description, then the body copy with clear section headers. Keep the copy ready to hand to a designer.
