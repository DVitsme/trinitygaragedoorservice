# Garage Door Replacement

Every garage door reaches a point where fixing it again stops making sense. Maybe you've had somebody out three times this year. Maybe it sounds like it's coming apart, or it's dented from a storm, or it just looks tired next to the rest of the house.

This page is about that decision: keep repairing, or replace. We'll help you make the honest call, and if a new door is the right move, we handle the whole swap.

## Repair or replace? How to tell

A lot of garage door problems are a quick fix. A broken spring, a frayed cable, a door off its track, a dead opener, we just repair those, often the same day. Replacing the whole door is a bigger decision, and these are the signs it's worth weighing.

**The repairs keep coming.** One fix is normal. But when you're calling every few months and the bills stack up, you're renting time on a door that's on its way out.

**It's old.** Doors last a long time with care, but parts fatigue and older doors miss modern safety features. If yours has been up there a couple of decades, it's fair to start thinking ahead.

**It's damaged.** Big dents, cracked or warped panels, rust, rot on a wood door, storm damage. Some of that is cosmetic and some of it actually weakens the door. We'll tell you which is which.

**It's loud.** Grinding, banging, or rattling can mean worn rollers or loose hardware, and sometimes a service call fixes it. But a door that's still loud after service is often just old and tired.

**The garage is always hot.** A thin, uninsulated door lets Florida heat pour straight in. If the garage bakes or your energy bill keeps creeping up, a modern insulated door makes a real difference.

**It looks dated.** If the door drags down the front of the house, replacing it is one of the quickest ways to freshen the whole exterior.

**It's not built for storms.** Older doors often aren't wind rated. In Tampa Bay the garage is the biggest opening on most houses, and an outdated door is a weak point when the wind picks up.

## What you actually gain

When replacing is the right move, here's what a new door buys you.

* **Quiet.** New rollers, fresh hardware, and a modern opener give you a door you barely hear from inside the house.
* **Lower heat and energy use.** An insulated door keeps the garage cooler and eases the load on your AC through the summer.
* **Better safety.** Newer doors have photo eye sensors and auto reverse, so the door stops and backs off if something's underneath it.
* **A door built for the weather.** You can move up to a wind rated door made for our storm season.
* **A fresh front.** A clean new door changes how the whole house looks from the curb.

## FAQs

**How do I know whether to repair or replace?**
Have us out for a free look. The rough rule: if the door has solid structure and one part failed, we repair it. If it's old, damaged, or you keep repairing it, replacing is usually the smarter money. We'll give you the real answer for your door.

**Can you reuse my existing opener?**
Often, yes, if it's in good shape. If it's worn out or you want smart features, we'll talk about a new one. Our opener repair and replacement service covers that.

**How long does a replacement take?**
Most are done in a day once your door is in. Custom or double doors can run a little longer, and we tell you that upfront.

**Do I need a permit to replace my door?**
In most of Tampa Bay, yes. The permit confirms the new door meets the wind rating for your area. We pull it and handle the inspection.

**Will a new door really lower my energy bill?**
If you're going from a thin uninsulated door to an insulated one, you'll feel it in the garage, especially in summer. We won't promise you a dollar figure, but the comfort gain is real and the AC works less to fight it.
