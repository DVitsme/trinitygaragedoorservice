A garage door never breaks at a convenient time. It's the morning you're already late, or the night you're trying to lock up the house, or right after a storm blows through. When that happens, you don't want a voicemail and a callback next week. You want a real person and a technician on the way.

## When it can't wait

Some garage door problems can sit until morning. These usually can't:

* **A broken spring with your car stuck inside.** When a spring snaps, the door becomes dead weight your opener can't lift, and your car is trapped behind it. This is our most common after hours call.
* **A door stuck open.** An open garage is an open house. Your tools, your cars, and everything in there are sitting out for the weather and anyone walking by. We'll get it closed and secure.
* **A door off the track.** A door hanging crooked or jammed in the opening can fall or do more damage every time it moves. Leave it alone and let us handle it. (More on our [off track repair](/services/repair/off-track/) page.)
* **A snapped cable.** The door drops on one side, hangs at an angle, and isn't safe to run.
* **Storm damage.** A door that took debris, got pushed in by wind, or won't close with weather coming.

## What to do until we get there

A few minutes of patience can save you a much bigger repair. While you wait on us:

* **Stop hitting the opener.** Forcing a jammed or unbalanced door can burn out the motor, snap a cable, or bend the panels and track. One or two tries is plenty.
* **Don't force a door with a broken spring or cable.** That's exactly when a door comes down hard. If you think a spring is gone, leave the door closed and call us.
* **Use the manual release carefully.** That red cord disconnects the door from the opener, but only pull it when the door is all the way down. Pulling it with the door up, or with a broken spring, can let the door crash. If the door feels really heavy or sits crooked after you release it, stop and wait for us.
* **Keep people, pets, and cars clear** of any door that's stuck, leaning, or partway open.

Hurricane season runs June through November, and storms keep us busy.
