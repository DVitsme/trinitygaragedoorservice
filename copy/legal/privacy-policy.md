# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [date you publish]

Trinity Garage Door Service, Inc. ("Trinity," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site or submitting a form, you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you fill out our contact or free estimate form, or otherwise reach out, we collect the details you provide. That typically includes your name, phone number, email address, city, the service you need, and any message you send. If you call or email us, we keep what you share so we can help you.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting platform records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. When you submit a form, we also record your IP address and browser details to help us prevent spam and abuse.

We do not collect payment card information through this website.

## How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

* respond to your request and answer your questions
* schedule and carry out garage door service
* follow up about your inquiry or completed work
* keep records of the work we do
* keep the site secure and prevent spam and fraud
* improve our website and service

## How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website and respond to you, and only as needed for them to do that work:

* **Cloudflare** hosts the website and stores form submissions in our database.
* **Cloudflare Turnstile** helps protect the form from spam and abuse.
* **Resend** delivers your form submission to our email inbox.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Trinity, our customers, or others.

## Where your information is stored

Form submissions are sent to us by email and stored in our database. Our service providers may process and store data on servers located in the United States.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses only the cookies needed for it to work and for spam protection. If we add website analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

## Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. You can also tell us to stop following up with you. To make a request, use the contact details below.

## Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

This website is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Links to other sites

Our site may link to other services, such as our online booking tool or social media pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those other sites, so please review their policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
