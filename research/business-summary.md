## 2. What they do (services)

Six core services, each with a dedicated page, plus a 24/7 emergency line:

1. **Installation** — new & replacement door installs; consultation → selection → install → final inspection. Sold on curb appeal, ROI, energy efficiency, security, lower maintenance.
2. **Repair & Service** — same-day repairs + preventive maintenance (lubrication, hardware tightening, spring/cable inspection, balance testing, safety-sensor checks).
3. **Off-Track Repair & Replacement** — assessment, track realignment, full track replacement, safety testing.
4. **Replacement** — 6-step "hassle-free" process; also à-la-carte parts (springs, cables, rollers, tracks, weatherstripping).
5. **Spring Repair & Replacement** — torsion + extension; offers **high-cycle oil-tempered springs**; cites 7–10 yr spring life. (Blog also pushes **black powder-coated springs** for Florida humidity/salt-air.)
6. **Opener Repair & Replacement** — diagnostics, motor/sensor/drive repair, smart/Wi-Fi integration; cites 10–15 yr opener life.

**Emergency positioning:** every page carries *"24/7 Garage Door Repair Service — Broken Springs, Cables, Rollers, Tracks and Openers."* (⚠️ slight tension with the stated 7am–9pm hours.)

## 5. Positioning & brand voice

- **Core promise:** honest, no-pressure, local, family-owned. *"The Most Trusted Name in Garage Doors. No shortcuts. No guesswork. Just skilled workmanship."* About page is deliberately anti-salesy: *"we don't really want to bore you with some sale spills… We're not like that."*
- **Differentiators (customer-validated in reviews):** no high-pressure sales, fair/competitive pricing, honest diagnosis, fast response (a flagship review: called 7am, on-site 8:45, fixed in 30 min), quote honored 9 months later.
- **Florida-resilience angle** is strong but lives **only in the blog**, not on the service pages: heat/humidity/salt-air wearing out springs, and **hurricane/storm-season** preparedness (the garage door as the home's overlooked structural weak point). Unifying this messaging across the main site is a clear opportunity.
- **Tone:** friendly, conversational, lightly playful (anthropomorphizes the door — *"your garage door trying to tell you something"*), mixing mild consequence/fear framing ("storm damage… thousands of dollars") with trust/reassurance.

## 10. Redesign watch-list (issues found during capture)

**Content gaps / opportunities**
- **Video is underused, not absent.** Only **2 videos** exist: a ~3-min promo of real Trinity technicians at work and a 13-sec finished-door showcase — both on About Us (the promo is also on Services). Neither is on the homepage, and the promo only plays from a click-to-open lightbox. Opportunity: put real footage on the homepage hero and expand into explainers ("meet the real team," noise diagnosis, manual-release-during-outage, hurricane bracing). See `videos.md`.
- **No pricing or financing transparency**; **no published warranty terms** (only "optional warranty packages" / "satisfaction guarantee").
- **Florida hurricane/heat angle absent from service pages** (only in the blog).
- **No FAQ/schema** despite lots of Q&A-style headings; reviews are **static/hard-coded** (will age).

**Quality / accuracy issues**
- **Founding year inconsistent:** 2007 vs. "15+ years" vs. 2011 (records).
- **Two different Tampa addresses** (14056 N Florida Ave 33613 vs. 4900 N Florida Ave Ste 301 33603); a **stray Pasco phone** (727) 388-7898 appears on one header; "dozen branches" claim vs. 3 listed locations.
- **Wayne Dalton** mislabeled as an opener (vs. door) brand.
- **Copy-paste errors:** spring page uses a "Replacement Services" contact heading; replacement page mislabels a bullet "Spring Replacement"; grammar/typo slips ("our honestly always goes a long way," "technicians specializes," "Without a properly functioning springs").

**Design-system issues**
- **All 229 images have empty alt text** — accessibility + SEO gap.
- **Imagery is generic:** AI-generated owner portraits, stock/AI product shots, reused logo files; **no real team headshots, storefront, or (mostly) job-site photography.**
- **Heavily templated copy:** an identical 8-review block, brand-logo strip, and "Connect with Us" form repeat across all service and brand pages; brand pages read as generic encyclopedia copy, not Trinity-specific.
