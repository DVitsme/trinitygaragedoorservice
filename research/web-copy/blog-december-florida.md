## Article Copy (verbatim)
December in Florida doesn't look like December anywhere else.

There's no snow to shovel. No iced-over driveways. But what we _do_ have is something just as demanding on your home: heavy holiday traffic, guests coming and going, packed schedules, road trips, deliveries, and doors that open and close more times in a single week than they did all month long.

And through all of it, one part of your home works overtime without ever getting much attention…your garage door.

At Trinity Garage Door Service, December is one of the busiest months of the year. Not because people suddenly care more about their garage doors, but because December has a way of revealing problems that were quietly building long before the holidays showed up.

### The Holiday Hustle Puts Real Strain on Garage Doors

Think about how much more your garage door handles this time of year:

• Guests arriving and leaving
• Kids home from school
• Packages being delivered daily
• Family visiting from out of town
• Extra errands, parties, church events
• Road trips and late nights

That means more cycles. More weight. More strain on springs, rollers, and openers.

A door that "worked fine most of the year" can suddenly start squeaking, lagging, shaking, or refusing to open altogether the moment it's under the most pressure.

And nobody wants to be dealing with a stuck garage door when guests are pulling into the driveway.

### The Two Issues We See the Most in December

There are two calls we get more than anything else this time of year:

#### 1. Broken Springs

Springs carry the full weight of your garage door. Over time, they weaken from repeated use and Florida humidity. One day they're fine… and the next day, the door won't budge.

Most homeowners don't realize their springs are near failure until they fail. And when they do, it's sudden, loud, and usually completely inconvenient.

#### 2. Failing or Noisy Openers

If your opener is straining, stalling, or waking up the whole house every time it runs, December is often when it finally waves the white flag.

Between heavier usage and older components reaching the end of their life, opener issues become much more noticeable this time of year.

### Why December Repairs Are About More Than Convenience

A malfunctioning garage door isn't just annoying. It affects:

• Your home's security
• Your family's safety
• Your ability to leave or return smoothly
• Your peace of mind when guests are staying
• Your confidence when travelling

And when something does go wrong, it rarely happens at a "convenient" time. It happens late at night, early in the morning, or right before you're trying to leave for something important.

That's why we always tell homeowners: it's better to be proactive in December than reactive in January.

Peace of mind isn't just about storms or emergencies.

In December, peace of mind means:
