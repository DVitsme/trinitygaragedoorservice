### Best-Selling Amarr Garage Doors
With a diverse selection of top-rated garage doors, Amarr offers models that combine strength, beauty, and innovation. Here are some of their most sought-after collections:

Designed to resemble the charm of wood carriage house doors, the Classica Collection is crafted from durable steel with high-definition stamped panels. #### Amarr Heritage Collection
For homeowners seeking durability and timeless design, the Heritage Collection features heavy-duty steel doors with low-maintenance finishes. Available in multiple colors and insulation options, these doors provide superior energy efficiency and noise reduction.

#### Amarr Lincoln Collection
The Lincoln Collection offers a balance of affordability and quality, making it a popular choice for homeowners looking for a durable garage door at a reasonable price. With a variety of panel styles, insulation choices, and window designs, this collection is versatile and stylish.

#### Amarr Horizon Collection
A perfect choice for modern homes, the Horizon Collection features sleek aluminum and glass panel doors. These full-view garage doors allow natural light to enter while maintaining privacy and security, making them a favorite among contemporary homeowners.

#### Amarr 2742 Commercial Garage Door
For industrial and commercial use, the Amarr 2742 is a heavy-duty ribbed steel door designed for high-traffic areas. With its wind-load reinforcement and insulation options, this door provides superior durability and protection.
