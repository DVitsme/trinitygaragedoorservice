### Top-Selling Wayne Dalton Garage Door Openers

#### Wayne Dalton iDrive Garage Door Opener
One of the most innovative models, the iDrive opener is a torsion-mounted system that eliminates the need for a traditional rail. This unique design frees up ceiling space and provides ultra-quiet operation.

#### Wayne Dalton ProDrive Garage Door Opener
A highly reliable and efficient choice, the ProDrive opener features DC-powered operation for smooth and quiet performance. It is ideal for homeowners who prioritize ease of use and smart technology compatibility.

#### Wayne Dalton Classic Drive Garage Door Opener
Designed for affordability and reliability, the Classic Drive model offers chain-driven performance with solid construction. It is a great option for those looking for a cost-effective yet powerful garage door opener.

#### Wayne Dalton Quantum Garage Door Opener
A top-tier model, the Quantum opener is known for its quiet belt-drive system, smart safety features, and Wi-Fi connectivity for remote control. This model is perfect for modern homes that require seamless automation.
