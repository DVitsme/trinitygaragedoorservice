**_Garage Door Repair Issues_**

Like other machines, garage doors are not 100% efficient. Here are some problems commonly encountered in the area of Lutz:

1. A common garage door repair issue is security. Though garage door safety has increased over the years, some glitches still exist. It is our responsibility to keep an eye out over some basic things including the state of our garage door parts. Several garage door repair issues stem out of worn and torn garage door parts.

2. Another common garage door repair issue concerns the door's sensor. Sensors ensure that any movement will stop the garage door from closing. This is very important because if someone ducks, or if the door senses that there is someone moving as the garage door is closing, it will immediately reverse. Problems arise when sensors become "insensitive."

3. Last in our list is the price. Indeed, the price of garage door repair services is an issue to some homeowners, especially when they are in a tight budget.
