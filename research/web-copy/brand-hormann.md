### A Brief History of Hörmann
Founded in 1935 by August Hörmann in Germany, Hörmann Group has grown into one of the world's largest manufacturers of garage doors, industrial doors, and entrance systems. With over 80 years of experience, the company has expanded globally, operating in more than 40 countries and manufacturing high-quality products that combine advanced German engineering with modern design elements.
