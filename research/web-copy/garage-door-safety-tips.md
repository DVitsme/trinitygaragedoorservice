### Safety Tips (13 total)

#### 1. Check Safety Sensors Regularly
Safety sensors are important features that are used to prevent the garage door from closing on an object. Found near the base of the door tracks, these sensors emit an invisible beam that detects objects in the door's path.
**Tip:** It is recommended to check your sensors once a month to make sure they are properly installed and cleaned. If your door fails to turn around when it hits an object, it is recommended to contact a professional such as Trinity Garage Door Service to have the device checked.

#### 2. Learn How to Use Manual Controls
Knowing how to manually operate your garage door in emergencies such as power outages or severe weather condition is essential.
**Tip:** Locate the red manual release cord, it is usually found attached to the garage door mechanism and can be spotted easily. Learn to identify the pull which deactivates the automatic system and then how to lift the door by hand.

#### 3. Master the Emergency Release Feature
The emergency release function can save lives if someone becomes trapped under a garage door.
**Tip:** It is recommended that all the adults in the household know how to use the emergency release, and it is advisable to check the release frequently. It is also important to make sure that the release works smoothly.

#### 4. Never Stand Under a Moving Garage Door
A garage door that is opening or closing can lead to serious injuries or even worst.
**Tip:** Make it a strict rule for everyone in the household that no one specially children should ever stand by the door when it is opening or closing. Do not attempt to enter or leave the garage until the door is fully open.

#### 5. Keep Garage Door Openers Out of Reach
Children are curious and may play with the garage door openers as if they are toys.
**Tip:** Deposit the remote controllers in safe places which are beyond the reach of children. Keep key fobs and wall controls out of the reach of children to prevent them from using them.

#### 6. Mount Wall Controls at a Safe Height
The wall controls should be placed in such a way that they cannot be used easily by children accidently.
**Tip:** Controls should be mounted at least 5 feet from the floor so that only adults can easily use them and children cannot reach them.

#### 7. Educate Children About Garage Door Safety
Garage doors are not toys. Misuse can lead to serious accidents.
**Tip:** Teach your children that the garage door should never be used for games or play. Emphasize the importance of staying clear of the moving door.

#### 8. Keep Fingers Away from Door Sections
Never depend on the garage door to close on its own.
**Tip:** Always supervise the door until it fully closes. This ensures that no children, pets, or belongings are caught under the closing door.

#### 9. Keep Fingers Away from Door Sections
Garage doors have seams through which sections meet and form points where one can easily get pinched.
**Tip:** Teach family members to keep their hands clear of door sections and other moving parts. Many modern doors come with pinch-resistant features, consider upgrading if yours does not, call Trinity Garage Door Services.

#### 10. Use the Door Handle
Handles are designed to make opening and closing the door safer.
**Tip:** Always use the designated handle to manually lift or lower the garage door instead of gripping the door edges.

#### 11. Reinforce the Garage Door
Reinforced garage doors offer greater protection against accidents, break-ins, and extreme weather.
**Tip:** Ensure your garage door is structurally sound and properly reinforced. Trinity Garage Doors can help reinforce doors to withstand wear and tear or even potential tampering.

#### 12. Keep Garage Code Secure
Neglecting garage security can compromise the safety of your home and loved ones.
**Tip:** Never share your garage access code unnecessarily. Regularly update your code to maintain security, especially if your garage connects directly to your home.

#### 13. Perform Regular Maintenance
Routine maintenance keeps your garage door functioning safely and efficiently.
**Tip:** Schedule an annual professional inspection with Trinity Garage Door Service to check springs, cables, rollers, and other components. Regular maintenance can prevent costly repairs and ensure your door remains safe to use.
