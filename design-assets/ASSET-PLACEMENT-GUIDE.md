## Handling rules (please follow)
1. **Real over stock:** wherever a slot matches a provided file, use the file — don't generate a stock substitute.
2. **Transparency:** `logo-*`, `badge-*`, and most `brandlogo-*` are transparent PNGs — place on any background.
3. **Orientation matters:** portrait job shots (`jobsite-*`, some `door-after-*`) suit tall IG tiles / cards; landscape team shots suit wide about blocks. Don't crop faces or door tops awkwardly.
4. **Placeholders to replace before launch:** `owner-...AI-PLACEHOLDER`, the low-res `doorstyle-*`, and (missing) real "before" photos.
5. **Brand palette** = the logo's red + near-black + steel-grey, on a warm off-white background. Pull the exact red from `logo-trinity-primary.png`.
6. Manufacturer logos/product shots are shown as an **authorized servicer** — fine for design comps.
